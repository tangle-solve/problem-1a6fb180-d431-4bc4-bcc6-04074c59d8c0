### CHEM-000474: Chromophore responsible for Sauvignon-blanc pinking
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Identify the chromophore responsible for the studied Sauvignon-blanc pinking and reproduce its behavior at native concentrations. Distinguish trace anthocyanins from alternative oxidized phenolic products.
- **Status:** Anthocyanins were established as a cause in Síria wines in 2014. The 2022 Sauvignon-blanc study remained inconclusive: its 1 mg/L detection limit exceeded the 0.3 mg/L visibility threshold reported for Síria.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Improves susceptibility testing and targeted prevention.
- **Key works:** A. P. Nel, W. J. du Toit, and F. P. van Jaarsveld (2022), “Attempted Identification of Causal Constituents of Pink Discolouration in White Wines,” [DOI: 10.21548/43-1-4812](https://doi.org/10.21548/43-1-4812); J. Andrea-Silva et al. (2014), “Origin of the pinking phenomenon of white wines,” [DOI: 10.1021/jf500825h](https://doi.org/10.1021/jf500825h).
- **Related:** Statistics—distinguishing absence from concentrations below detection limits.
